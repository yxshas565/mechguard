# MechGuard — Current Status

> **Stage: Research Prototype / Early MVP**
>
> MechGuard is an experimental mechanistic AI assurance framework investigating internal model dynamics during fine-tuning and internal coordination signals during multi-agent deployment.

---

## Evidence Policy

MechGuard explicitly distinguishes between:

- **Implemented** — functionality currently present in the repository.
- **Literature-supported** — findings established by external research.
- **Reproduced** — external methods/results independently reproduced by MechGuard.
- **Experimental** — results generated by MechGuard's own experiments.
- **Hypothesis** — research questions currently being tested.
- **Roadmap** — planned product capabilities not yet implemented.

We do not present literature results, synthetic demonstrations, or proposed capabilities as MechGuard experimental results.

## 🔵 Literature-Grounded

The current research direction is grounded in recent work on:

- Emergent misalignment from narrow fine-tuning
- Model organisms for emergent misalignment
- Convergent internal representations
- Trait-space monitoring
- Persona/subspace structure associated with emergent misalignment
- Internal activation-based detection of multi-agent collusion
- Latent representation structure in multi-agent systems
- Limitations and potential obfuscation of internal detectors

See `CITATIONS.md` for the research foundation.

## 🟠 Currently Being Validated

### Study A

- Whether optimization geometry changes systematically during emergent misalignment.
- Whether a commutator-defect signal is predictive of emergent misalignment.
- Whether internal geometric signals precede behavioral evidence.
- Whether relevant low-dimensional directions/subspaces remain stable across seeds and model configurations.

### Study B

- Reproduction of activation-based multi-agent collusion monitoring on released benchmark data.
- Robustness under distribution shift.
- Whether activation signals distinguish learned coordination from generic base-model representation geometry.
- Whether causal activation intervention changes measured coordination behavior.

### Training → Deployment Bridge

The central MechGuard research hypothesis is:

> **Training-time internal changes may provide evidence predictive of downstream safety risk during deployment.**

This relationship has not yet been established by MechGuard and is the primary research question being investigated.

## ⚪ Not Yet Demonstrated by MechGuard

The following must NOT be interpreted as MechGuard experimental results unless explicitly moved into the experimental-results section with reproducible evidence:

- A fixed "125-step" early-warning result
- MechGuard-specific 0.990 AUROC
- MechGuard-specific 1.00 AUROC
- A causal training-to-deployment safety link
- Causal proof that internal geometric changes cause downstream coordination
- Production latency guarantees
- Production-scale runtime monitoring
- vLLM integration
- OpenTelemetry integration
- Signed model attestation
- Immutable evidence storage
- Automated compliance-report generation
- Enterprise customer deployments
- Revenue or customer traction
