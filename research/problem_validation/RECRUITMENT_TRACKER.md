# Candidate Qualification

Before outreach, record why the candidate is relevant.

## Strong qualification

The candidate has direct experience with one or more of:

1. Fine-tuning or adapting models.
2. Running model evaluations.
3. Building evaluation infrastructure.
4. AI safety or security testing.
5. Model deployment validation.
6. Model governance or risk.
7. ML platform / model registry infrastructure.

## Weak qualification

The candidate only:

1. Writes about AI.
2. Studies AI academically without relevant workflow exposure.
3. Works in generic software engineering.
4. Has only theoretical governance knowledge.
5. Has no model lifecycle responsibility.

Weak candidates may be useful for context but should not dominate the
interview sample.

# Recruitment Quality Checks

Before concluding recruitment, verify:

1. Multiple practitioner roles represented.
2. Multiple organizations represented.
3. Production experience represented.
4. Different transformation workflows represented.
5. Different evaluation maturity levels represented.
6. At least some candidates can provide contradictory evidence.
7. Recruitment is not dominated by people already familiar with MechGuard.
8. Research participants are not selected solely because they agree with
   the problem hypothesis.

---

# Privacy

Only record professional information necessary for recruitment and research.

Do not record:

1. Personal sensitive information.
2. Private credentials.
3. Confidential company information.
4. Information unrelated to the research.

Respect any request from a participant to withdraw their information.
